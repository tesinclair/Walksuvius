## A key to the colour of a tagged cube

Use `walksuvius view TAGGED:out/log/cubes_TAGGED` to see the outputted tags
note since all cubes are greyscale the TAGGED key MUST be used to switch
on the colour coding.

# Key

- Normal Blob: {5, 201, 255, 255}
- Suspicious Blob with no junction: {180, 5, 255, 255}
- Suspicious blob with a suspected bridge: {251, 255, 5, 255}
- Bridge Centre pixel: {5, 26, 255, 255}
- Area to cut: {166, 27, 17, 255}
